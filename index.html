Install on Android

Shut Up Brain is installed directly as an APK. Android may show security prompts because the app is downloaded outside the Google Play Store.

  1. Tap Download for Android.
  2. When the APK finishes downloading, tap the downloaded file.
  3. If Android asks permission to install unknown apps from your browser, open Settings and allow it for that browser.
  4. Return to the APK and tap Install.
  5. Google Play Protect may scan the APK before installation. Let the scan finish.
  6. Tap Open and launch Shut Up Brain.
That warning is normal. Android warns users before installing apps that did not come through the Play Store. This page links to the official Shut Up Brain APK.
