Updating on Android
  1. Tap Download Latest Update.
  2. Open the downloaded APK when it finishes.
  3. Android should ask whether you want to update Shut Up Brain. Tap Update or Install.
  4. Google Play Protect may scan the update. Let the scan finish.
  5. Open Shut Up Brain normally. Your local app data should remain in place.
